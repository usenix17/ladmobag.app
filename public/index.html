The most coveted prize in Arizona

For 35 years, The Wallace and Ladmo Show ran on KPHO Channel 5 in Phoenix, and kids across the Valley watched it before and after school. It was one of the longest-running children’s shows in American television.

Ladmo handed out a plain brown paper sack stuffed with candy, toys, and treats. Kids in the studio audience won them, and kids at home hoped to hear their names called. Getting a Ladmo Bag was a big deal. Plenty of grown Arizonans still remember the day they got theirs.

About this replica

I won a real Ladmo Bag, and it lives in my safe. It isn’t going anywhere, so I recreated the lettering instead. Now anyone can make one.

Make one for a birthday, a Phoenix-themed party, or a friend who grew up watching the show and still talks about it.

Make your own

Artwork: 5.00 × 10.00 in · 360 × 720 pt
  1. Download the PDF

    The lettering is already outlined, so it prints the same on any computer.

  2. Print at 100%

    Turn off “fit to page.” The artwork fits on letter or A4 paper.

  3. Put it on a sack

    Use a brown paper lunch sack. If your printer has a straight manual feed, print right on the flattened sack. Otherwise print on paper, trim, and glue it on.

  4. Fill it

    Candy, a whistle, a yo-yo, a coupon for a burger. Fold the top over and hand it out.
